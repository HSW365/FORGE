The Offer Equation

A great offer isn't a lower price — it's a higher perceived value against a lower perceived cost. FORGE runs every offer through one equation:

Value = (Dream Outcome × Belief it'll work) ÷ (Time it takes × Effort it takes)

Your job in the next modules is to push the top of that equation up and the bottom down until saying "no" feels irrational. Most founders only ever touch price. You're about to touch all four levers.

Module 02 · Open

Find the Painful Problem

You don't get paid for what you make. You get paid for the problem you remove. Before any offer, FORGE forces one answer: what expensive, urgent, recurring pain does this end?

  • Expensive — it's already costing them money, time, or status.
  • Urgent — it hurts now, not "someday."
  • Recurring — it comes back until someone solves it for good.

Score your problem 1–5 on each. Under 10 out of 15, you don't have an offer yet — you have a feature. Fix that here before you build anything.

Module 03 · Open

Value Stacking

One deliverable is a product. A stack is an offer. FORGE teaches you to break the transformation into the pieces the buyer would otherwise have to assemble alone — and hand them all of it in one place.

  • The core result (the thing they came for)
  • The shortcut (what saves them weeks)
  • The safety net (what removes the risk)
  • The proof (why they believe you)

Price the stack against the cost of not solving the problem — never against your hours. That reframe is where the next eleven modules live.
